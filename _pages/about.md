## 👋 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student in Geography at the University of Wisconsin–Madison**, affiliated with the **[Spatial Computing and Data Mining Lab](https://scdm.geography.wisc.edu/)**. I completed my M.S. in Geography (Cartography/GIS, thesis) at UW–Madison in May 2026 and am also pursuing an **M.S. in Computer Sciences**.

My research focuses on **geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI) and remote sensing for environmental hazards**, particularly **active-fire detection, wildfire-progression forecasting, and post-fire burned-area and damage assessment**. I integrate multi-source satellite observations with weather, terrain, and land-cover data to develop spatiotemporal and physically informed learning methods.

I also work on **hyperspectral reconstruction, remote-sensing super-resolution, and hydrological time-series modeling**, using transformers, graph neural networks, and multimodal deep learning. My goal is to develop generalizable and interpretable Earth-observation methods that support environmental monitoring and decision-making.

### 🔬 Current Research Topics

- **Wildfire early detection:** combining GOES and VIIRS observations with remote-sensing super-resolution and active-fire detection.
- **Wildfire-progression forecasting:** modeling multi-step fire-front evolution with satellite observations and environmental conditions.
- **Post-fire mapping:** burned-area and burn-severity assessment using bi-temporal imagery and attention-based models.
- **Hyperspectral reconstruction:** recovering spectral and spatial detail from sparse multispectral observations.
- **Hydrological modeling:** learning spatial and temporal dependencies in river networks for streamflow imputation and prediction.
